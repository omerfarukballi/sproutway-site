Frequently asked

On your mind

Does Sprout Way compare my baby with other babies?

No. There are no rankings, charts or comparisons with other babies in Sprout Way. The only comparison is with your baby's own yesterday: the map shows how far they have come on their very own journey.

Is this a medical app?

No. Sprout Way is not a medical device and is not a substitute for professional advice. The content is for information and support; for any questions about your child, please consult a qualified professional.

Does it use artificial intelligence?

No. Sprout Way does not use AI. Every suggestion is deterministic and reasoned: it is grounded in developmental science (Piaget, Vygotsky, Ayres, Harvard Serve & Return) and runs on content that has passed review by a child development specialist. You can always see why something is suggested.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can start using Sprout Way without an email or an account. And when you do want backup and sync, those are free as well.

What is included in the free version?

Everything you use today is free: the day's suggestion, teachable moments, activities, celebrations, unlimited records and archive, backup and sync. Premium unlocks the full path: map depth (the details and the science of the steps ahead), lenses, Life Questions and Curiosity depth, Insights, a second child profile, themes and watermark-free sharing.
